01Who joins

Cardiovascular therapeutics companies.

Companies working across cardiovascular development and care.

Independent scientific, clinical, regulatory, and health-system organizations join individual projects as invited partners. They are not a membership tier.

02How it works

Members decide. The team carries it out with independent partners.

  • Member companies

    One company, one equal vote. Members elect the Board and choose the shared priorities.

  • Board of Directors

    Elected from member-company representatives. Governs the association and is forming with the founding membership.

  • PrecisionCardio team

    Runs the work, overseen by the Board.

  • Collective action

    Approved initiatives in regulatory science, clinical research, care delivery, and health-system decision-making, carried out with independent partners.

Voting, committee, and governance provisions will be set out in the governing documents and membership materials.

03Dues and taking part

Dues sustain the institution.

Member dues sustain the scientific, strategic, legal, and operational capacity to advance member-selected priorities. Larger evidence projects are scoped separately and funded by the members who take part in them.

  • Raise the barriers you are seeing across the field.
  • Vote on shared priorities and the budget.
  • Contribute expertise to approved initiatives.
04Boundaries

At the disease and system level, the challenge is shared.

Product-specific decisions are not.

Each company keeps its own product-specific development decisions, confidential trial data, proprietary strategies, and product-specific pricing, contract, and coverage decisions. Collective work runs under an antitrust protocol being developed with counsel. See the full boundary →
