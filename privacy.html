Privacy policy

How PrecisionCardio collects, uses and protects the personal information you share with us.

Effective September 16, 2026  ·  Last updated September 24, 2026

1. Introduction and scope

PrecisionCardio Industry Alliance, Inc. (“PrecisionCardio,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) is a registered 501(c)(6) nonprofit tax-exempt organization committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, disclose, and protect personal information through:

  • Our website(s) located at precisioncardio.org (the “Website”);
  • Our events, conferences, webinars, and programs (the “Events”);
  • Our membership, donation, and fundraising platforms; and
  • Other communications and services we offer (collectively, the “Services”).

This Privacy Policy applies to all information collected through your interactions with us, whether electronic, written, or oral. We process your information as described in this policy. Where your consent is required for a specific activity, such as the collection of sensitive health data, certain types of tracking, SMS communications, research participation, or the publication of your likeness, we will request that consent separately and specifically at the point of collection.

Where a specific program, registry, research study, or service has its own privacy notice, consent form, or HIPAA authorization, that specific document controls for that activity. This Privacy Policy governs all other data practices.

2. Who we are

PrecisionCardio Industry Alliance, Inc. is a pharmaceutical industry special interest alliance organization with a keen focus on precision cardiovascular medicine. For privacy-related questions, you may contact us at privacy@precisioncardio.org.

3. Information we collect

We collect personal information only when you choose to submit it through our membership form. The form asks for your first and last name, email address, job title, and company, along with any message you choose to include. We do not ask or collect demographic information or any medical information, nor do we ask or collect your financial and payment information.

If you interact with us through social media platforms, the social media website and/or we may collect information such as social media identifiers and profile information and information about your activity related to our organization, for example, photos, tags, etc.

To the extent you may register for or attend one of our hosted events, we may collect your information relevant to event registration and participation such as your name, your email address, job title or name of your company.

We do not use cookies or advertising trackers on this site. Like most websites, our hosting provider, form server and general analytics tools may keep standard server logs, such as aggregated and de-identified information regarding visit stats. We do not use those logs to identify you or to provide targeted advertisements to you.

Finally, we may aggregate or de-identify collected information so that it does not identify an individual. We commit not to attempt to re-identify de-identified data except as permitted by applicable law. Where we share de-identified data with third parties, we require recipients to maintain the de-identified status and not to attempt re-identification. De-identified and aggregated data may be used and shared for analytics, research, and other purposes.

The site loads its fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser shares your IP address with Google when a page loads. Google handles that information under its own privacy policy.

4. How we collect information

We collect your Personal Information through the following methods:

  • Information you provide directly: When you register for or renew membership, Events, or programs; make a donation; complete a survey; submit a form; contact us; or otherwise interact with us.
  • Information from third parties: From social media platforms, service providers, partners, publicly available sources, and commercially available sources.
  • Information collected at Events: When you attend conferences, webinars, or fundraising events, including through badge scanning, registration, or virtual meeting platforms.
5. How we use information

We use the information you submit to respond to your inquiry, to talk with you about membership, and, if your company joins or renews, to administer that membership. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising. To the extent you elect to register or participate in any of the events, conferences and webinars, we may use your information to administer such participation.

We may occasionally also send newsletters, updates and informational communications, sometimes about industry news, sometimes about upcoming events, conferences and webinars. To the extent you elect to opt into receiving such information, we will use that information to contact you. You may opt out of such communications at any time by:

  • Following the unsubscribe link in any email;
  • Replying “STOP” to any text message;
  • Contacting us at privacy@precisioncardio.org.

Occasionally, we may further use aggregated and de-identified information to conduct data analytics to better understand the communities we serve.

Finally, we may use your information to facilitate participation in advocacy campaigns to support public policy related to the research and development efforts for the industry.

We do not use artificial intelligence (AI) and machine learning technologies to interact with you, and will not use your information in any AI model training.

6. How we disclose information

We may share Personal Information with third-party service providers who perform functions on our behalf, including:

  • Hosting and data storage
  • Collecting membership information and payment processing
  • Email and communication services
  • Database management
  • Customer service and support
  • Analytics and research services
  • Event management and registration
  • Fulfillment and shipping
  • Marketing and fundraising assistance
  • As required by law

Service providers are subject to confidentiality obligations and may use Personal Information only to provide services to us.

We do not sell any Personal Information provided by you to any third-party advertisers and analytics companies. This may include information collected through cookies and similar technologies for targeted advertising purposes.

7. User-generated content, testimonials, and patient stories

If you submit content, including testimonials, patient stories, photographs, videos, or other materials, to us, via email or social media, you grant PrecisionCardio a nonexclusive, perpetual, irrevocable, worldwide, sublicensable, transferable, royalty-free right and license to use, store, display, publish, transmit, distribute, reproduce, edit, redact, modify, aggregate, summarize, adapt, create derivative works, and publicly perform the content.

For photographs, videos, patient stories, or testimonials that will be used publicly, in marketing materials, on our Website, in social media, or in publications, we may require a separate media release or consent form signed by you. The privacy policy’s content license does not replace this separate consent.

8. Email, newsletters, and fundraising communications

To the extent you opt in to receive such communication, we may send you newsletters and educational content and event invitations and updates as requested. You may opt out of receiving communications from us at any time by:

  • Clicking the unsubscribe link at the bottom of any email;
  • Replying “STOP” to any text message;
  • Updating your communication preferences at your membership portal;
  • Contacting us at privacy@precisioncardio.org.
9. Your privacy choices and rights

You have the following choices regarding your Personal Information:

  • Access: You may request a copy of the Personal Information we hold about you.
  • Correction: You may request correction of inaccurate or outdated information.
  • Deletion: You may request deletion of your Personal Information.
  • Opt-out: You may opt out of certain uses of your information, including marketing communications, targeted advertising, the sale or sharing of your Personal Information, and profiling.
  • Withdraw consent: Where we process your information based on consent, you may withdraw that consent at any time.
  • Data portability: You may request an electronic copy of your Personal Information in a portable format.
  • Appeal: If we refuse to act on your request, you may appeal by contacting privacy@precisioncardio.org.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@precisioncardio.org. We will respond to your request within 14 days, or as required by applicable law. We may need to verify your identity before processing your request.

10. Children’s privacy

Our Website and Services are not directed to children under the age of 16. You may not use our Services if you are under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect, solicit, or maintain Personal Information from anyone under the age of 16. If we learn that we collected Personal Information from a child under 16 without verified parental consent, we will use commercially reasonable efforts to delete the information.

11. Data retention

We may store the information we collect about you for as long as we need it to respond to you and to manage any membership that follows. If you ask us to delete it, we will, unless the law requires us to keep it.

12. International visitors

Our Website and Services are not intended specifically to address international visitors. Information may be transferred to, processed in, and stored in the United States and other countries where we or our service providers operate. Privacy laws in those countries may differ from and may not be as protective as those in your country.

13. Third-party links

Our Website may contain links to third-party websites, services, or embedded content. We do not control the privacy practices of these third parties and are not responsible for their content or practices. We do not endorse information, products, or services provided on third-party websites.

Embedded content (e.g., videos, images, articles) from third-party websites behaves as though you had visited the third-party website directly. Third parties may collect data, use cookies, embed additional tracking, and monitor interactions.

We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party websites you visit.

14. Changes to this privacy policy

This Privacy Policy may be periodically updated or modified. Changes will be posted on this page, and the date of the most recent revision will be noted at the top of this policy. Your continued use of our Services after changes are posted constitutes your agreement to the updated Privacy Policy with regard to any information collected after the changes take effect. You are responsible for checking our Website periodically for changes.

15. Accessibility

We are committed to making our Website and Services accessible to all users, including those with disabilities. If you have difficulty accessing or using any part of our Website or Services, or if you have suggestions for improving accessibility, please contact us at legal@precisioncardio.org.

16. Contact us

If you have questions, comments, or concerns about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us at privacy@precisioncardio.org.

Questions about your information? Email privacy@precisioncardio.org.
