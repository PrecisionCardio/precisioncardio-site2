Privacy

Privacy policy

PrecisionCardio Industry Alliance, Inc. (“PrecisionCardio,” “we,” or “us”) operates precisioncardio.org. This policy explains what personal information we collect through the site, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Effective September 16, 2026

What we collect

We collect personal information only when you choose to submit it through our membership form. The form asks for your name, email address, job title, and company, along with any message you choose to include.

We do not use cookies, analytics, or advertising trackers on this site. Like most websites, our hosting provider keeps standard server logs, such as IP address, browser type, and the time of a visit, for security and to keep the site running. We do not use those logs to identify you.

The site loads its fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser shares your IP address with Google when a page loads. Google handles that information under its own privacy policy.

How we use it

We use the information you submit to respond to your inquiry, to talk with you about membership, and, if your company joins, to administer that membership. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Who we share it with

We share your information only with service providers that host the site, run the membership form, and provide our email, and only as far as they need it to provide those services. We may also disclose information when the law requires it, or as part of a reorganization or dissolution of PrecisionCardio.

How long we keep it

We keep your information for as long as we need it to respond to you and to manage any membership that follows. If you ask us to delete it, we will, unless the law requires us to keep it.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us at any time to see the information we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it, and you can ask us to stop contacting you. Email us at privacy@precisioncardio.org and we will respond within 30 days.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, we process your information because you asked us to respond to you and because we have a legitimate interest in discussing membership with organizations that contact us. You also have the right to object to that processing, to ask us to restrict it, to receive your information in a portable format, and to complain to your local data protection authority. Your information is stored in the United States.

Security

We use reasonable measures to protect the information you submit, and only the people at PrecisionCardio who need it can see it. No method of storing or transmitting information is completely secure.

Children

This site is for professionals and is not directed to anyone under 16. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version on this page and update the effective date.
